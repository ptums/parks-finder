## Oddities that matter

1. **Images never load.** All 12 image URLs are on `images.example.com` (a reserved domain that won't serve images). Every image the app renders will hit the failure path. The placeholder is the normal case, not an edge case.
2. **Hours are free text**: `"6:00 AM - 1:00 AM"` (crosses midnight), `"Dawn to dusk"`, `"24 hours"`, plus several `H:MM AM - H:MM PM` ranges. Show verbatim; no "open now" computation.
3. **Suspicious coordinate**: `cedar-hill-nature-preserve` is at `40.7128, -74.0060`, which is the commonly used "New York City" default point (lower Manhattan, City Hall area), not a nature preserve. Likely placeholder data. Show it as given; note in README assumptions.
4. **Location**: all parks fall in the NYC area (lat 40.58-40.85, lng -74.09 to -73.79). Some are real NYC parks (Prospect Park), most are fictional. Municipality choice: "NYC-area (fictional mix)".
5. **Description duplicates fields**: e.g., Prospect Park's description says "526-acre", also in `acreage`. No conflict found.
